## Part 2: Game Overview (Public — Safe to Share Before Play)
- **Setting & Historical Period**: Contemporary 2020s. The MV Meridian, a large cross-channel ferry, is stranded in the English Channel. Dense winter fog has reduced visibility to zero. The ship’s engines are idling.
- **Players**: 5 Players + 1 DM
- **Tone & Difficulty**: Tense claustrophobic psychological suspense. Medium difficulty.
- **Basic Background**: You are five strangers bound together by circumstance on a frozen sea. The air is thick with moisture and unease. The ship’s manifest says there are fifty passengers. But when the crew conducted a emergency headcount due to the fog, they counted fifty-one souls. Someone is not who they seem. Or someone is here who shouldn’t be. Then, a scream—or perhaps just a loud bang—echoed from the lower decks. Now, the lights flicker, the fog presses against the windows, and trust is the first thing lost overboard.
- **The Central Incident**: A discrepancy in the passenger count ("One Person Too Many") coincided with a strange noise from the Engine Room sector. When searched, the area was empty, save for a lingering smell of saltwater and rust. The ship’s communication systems received a single call from an "Unknown Number" minutes prior.
- **Player Characters**:
  - **P1 — Sarah Jenkins (Passenger)**: Anxious, well-dressed, checking her watch constantly.
  - **P2 — Leo Vance (Steward)**: Jittery, uniform slightly disheveled, avoiding eye contact.
  - **P3 — Elena Ross (Journalist)**: Sharp-eyed, notebook in hand, watching everyone closely.
  - **P4 — Marcus Thorne (Deckhand)**: Broad-shouldered, quiet, arms crossed defensively.
  - **P5 — Benji Cole (Engineer)**: Greasy hands, pragmatic, frustrated by the halt.

## Part 3: DM / Host Guide (DM ONLY)

### 3.1 Starting Situation & Setup
- Distribute Character Booklets (Part 6).
- Announce: "It is 00:30. The fog is impenetrable. The Captain has ordered everyone to the Lounge for a recount. We found one extra person. And then, silence from the lower decks."
- Release **Start Clues**: C1 (Duty Rota) and C4 (Manifest) are public.

### 3.2 Staged Round-by-Round Flow
- **Round 1 — Surface Alibis** (~20 min):
  - *Event*: Players discuss where they were at 00:15.
  - *Clues released*: C2 (Security Cam - blurry), C3 (Call Record - given to P2), C5 (Wet Pocket - P2 discovers it on himself).
  - *Guidance*: Encourage P2 to panic about the wet pocket. Let P1 hint she saw movement.
- **Round 2 — Escalation & Secrets** (~25 min):
  - *Event*: Power fluctuates. A radio message comes through: "We need to check the Engine Room."
  - *Clues released*: C6 (Business Card - P3 finds it in her pocket), C7 (Patrol Log - P4 tries to show it to deflect), C8 (Signal Trace - P5 gets it from engineer console).
  - *Guidance*: Force interaction between P3 and P4. If P4 lies about C7, allow others to question it.
- **Round 3 — Confrontation** (~20 min):
  - *Event*: The Captain demands a final vote on who is responsible for the security breach/hiding the truth.
  - *Clues released*: C9 (Passport - P1 realizes she has it), C10 (Pump Code - P5 confirms timing).
  - *Guidance*: Players must combine C2 (Figure), C8 (Location), C10 (Time) to realize Julian was in the Engine Room, not Deck B.

### 3.3 Special Rules
- **Whisper Mode**: Players may whisper privately to trade info.
- **No Violence**: This is a mystery of concealment, not murder. The goal is truth, not punishment.

### 3.4 Ending Determination & Winner Scoring Rules
1. **How to Determine the Story Ending**:
   - **Ending A**: Accusation against P4 + Discovery of Body/Phone location.
   - **Ending B**: Accusation against P2 or P5, or no accusation.
   - **Ending C**: P1 admits bribery/helping Julian AND P3 exposes P4.
   - **Ending D**: Tie vote or refusal to decide.
2. **Personal Objective & Winner Resolution Table**:

| Player | Primary Objective (2 pts) | Secondary Objective (1 pt) | Exact Pass Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| P1 (Sarah) | Protect secret of helping Julian | Clear name of murder | Primary: Not accused of killing. Secondary: Not identified as accomplice to boarding. |
| P2 (Leo) | Hide bribe | Avoid blame for noise | Primary: Bribe money not found. Secondary: Not accused of causing the incident. |
| P3 (Elena) | Expose Marcus | Get Scoop | Primary: Marcus identified as concealer. Secondary: Evidence of fraud presented. |
| P4 (Marcus) | Keep Julian hidden | Maintain Alibi | Primary: No one finds the body/phone. Secondary: Not voted guilty. |
| P5 (Benji) | Prove machine safety | Find cause of noise | Primary: Not blamed for mechanical failure. Secondary: Correctly identifies time of event. |

3. **Winner Rule**: Highest points wins. If P4 reaches Primary+Secondary, he wins (Escape). If P3 reaches Primary, she wins (Justice).

## Part 5: Final Solution (DM ONLY)
- **The Central Truth**: Julian Vane, a fugitive, boarded illegally. He called the Galley (C3) asking for a way off. He ran toward the Engine Room (C2) to hide. Marcus Thorne (P4), who owed Julian money or feared exposure, saw him. Julian slipped into the open bilge hatch while fleeing. The pump tried to activate (C10) but jammed on his body. Marcus retrieved Julian’s phone (dropped during fall) and hid it, then returned to Deck B to maintain his rota alibi (C1, C7).
- **Responsible Party / Cause**: Marcus Thorne (Concealment/Negligence). Julian Vane (Accidental Death).
- **Motive / Underlying Conflict**: Marcus feared being linked to Julian’s crimes.
- **Method / Mechanism**: Fall into bilge pump intake.
- **Reconstructed Canonical Timeline**:
  - 00:00: Julian calls Galley (C3).
  - 00:10: Julian runs past Cam (C2).
  - 00:15: Julian falls into Bilge (C10). Marcus witnesses.
  - 00:20: Marcus hides phone.
  - 00:30: Headcount discrepancy (C4).
- **Clue-to-Conclusion Proof Chain**:
  1. C4 + C9 prove Julian was the extra person.
  2. C2 places Julian in the corridor at 00:10.
  3. C8 places the phone (and thus Julian) in the Engine Room sector.
  4. C10 proves something blocked the pump at 00:15.
  5. C1 vs C7: Marcus claimed he was on Deck B, but C2/C8 place the incident elsewhere. Only Marcus could have moved the phone/body evidence, as he was the only one with access to both Deck B and Engine Room corridors during the gap.
  6. C6 explains Marcus’s motive (past association with Elena/Julian circle).
- **Accounting for Red Herrings**: C5 (Wet Pocket) was coffee. C7 (Log) was forged.
